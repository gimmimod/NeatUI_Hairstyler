description=[h1]💇 NeatUI Hairstyler[/h1]
description=Choosing a haircut shouldn't mean scrolling a drop-down menu. See every style on your own character, side by side, and pick the one you like. 🧡
description=
description=[h2]✨ What you get[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] 🖼️ Every hairstyle and beard shown on your character, in a grid
description=[*] 🔄 Turn any preview with the mouse to see it from the side or the back
description=[*] 👕 Previews show your hair, not your hat — no need to undress first
description=[*] 🎨 Any hair colour you want, not just the handful the game offers
description=[*] 🔍 Too many styles? Show vanilla only, or one mod at a time
description=[*] 📏 Drag the corner to make the grid as big or as small as you like
description=[*] 🕹️ Works with a controller
description=[*] 🌍 Translated into every language the game speaks
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🖱️ How to use it[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] In character creation, the [b]scissors[/b] and [b]razor[/b] buttons open the grid
description=[*] In game, cut your hair or trim your beard as usual
description=[*] Click a style to wear it — the window stays open, so try several in a row
description=[*] Hold and drag on a preview to turn your character
description=[*] [b]Drag the header[/b] to move the grid aside and watch your character while you browse
description=[*] [b]Drag the corner[/b] to resize: the previews reflow as you go
description=[*] The [b]coloured square[/b] opens the colour picker
description=[*] The red [b]X[/b] closes the grid
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]⚙️ Settings[/h2]
description=[b]CONTROLS[/b], right there in the panel: the style name on hover instead of in the header, turning previews with the mouse, previews with or without clothes, turn one or turn them all, and the full colour picker or the game's small palette.
description=
description=[b]Options → Mods → NeatUI Hairstyler[/b]: [b]Resize by dragging[/b] (on by default — turn it off for Rows and Columns counters instead), plus the colour of the style you are wearing and the colour of the style selector.
description=
description=Whatever you choose is remembered.
description=
